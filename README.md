# Legacy Exterior Shine

Build a high-converting, fully responsive, professional mobile-first website for a local trade business named "Legacy Exterior Services LLC". 

The website must be clean, minimalistic, family-friendly, and calm—avoiding any cold, robotic, cyber-punky, or overly flashy agency styles. It should feel warm, grounded, trustworthy, and approachable, like a dependable local neighbor.

=== BRAND IDENTITY & ASSETS ===

- Business Name: Legacy Exterior Services LLC

- Phone Number: (214) 205-4075 (Click-to-call enabled across all key touchpoints)

- Service Area: Dallas-Fort Worth (DFW) Metroplex (Plano, Frisco, Dallas, Allen, McKinney)

- Logo: Extract and display the uploaded professional minimalist logo (featuring clean typography and exterior service vibe) prominently in the sticky header.

- Reviews / Rating: 4.5-star aggregate rating based on 16 Google reviews. Extract and display customer reviews from the provided business profile file/link.

=== COLOR PALETTE & STYLING ===

- Background & Containers: Clean light off-white background (#F8FAFC) paired with soft, clean card containers.

- Primary Brand & Headers: Deep Slate Navy (#1E293B) for navigation, text headers, and structural sections to project corporate reliability and strength.

- Accent / Action Pop: Rich Amber / Warm Wood Gold (#D97706) strictly reserved for high-priority conversion elements: "Call Now" buttons, "Get Free Quote" buttons, rating stars, and active interactive states. 

- Typography: Use a clean, friendly sans-serif font family (such as Plus Jakarta Sans or Inter) with high legibility, generous whitespace, and soft dark slate text (#334155) for body paragraphs.

=== SITE STRUCTURE & NAVIGATION ===

Extract all text copy, headings, and detailed descriptions directly from the uploaded text file and map them across the following structure:

1. STICKY HEADER:

   - Logo on the left, click-to-call phone number ((214) 205-4075) and primary "Get Free Quote" button on the right. Stays fixed on scroll.

2. HERO SECTION:

   - Location badge, clear value proposition headline, engaging subheadline, and dual conversion buttons (Amber "Call Now" and Outline/Navy "Request Free Online Estimate"). Include a high-impact before-and-after visual container.

3. TRUST & AUTHORITY STRIP:

   - Clean iconography highlighting local experience, fully insured status, and the 4.5-star DFW homeowner rating.

4. CORE SERVICES GRID:

   - Clean cards for the 4 core offerings:

     * Fence & Deck Staining

     * Pressure Washing & Surface Cleaning

     * Exterior House Soft-Washing

     * Wood Restoration & Prep

   - Each card features a summary snippet and a [Learn More] button.

5. DEDICATED SUB-PAGES / EXPANDABLE SECTIONS (Linked via "Learn More"):

   - When users click [Learn More] on a service card, navigate them to its dedicated sub-page (or render an optimized view) featuring the deep-dive problem/solution copy from the text file.

   - Every sub-page MUST include an explicit navigation anchor: [← Back to Main Services] at the top, followed by inline conversion blocks (Call Now + Free Estimate form trigger).

6. ABOUT US SUB-PAGE:

   - A dedicated page outlining the company's local DFW roots, commitment to meticulous prep work, and core philosophy. Includes a [← Back to Home] link and conversion block.

7. BEFORE & AFTER VISUAL GALLERY:

   - Side-by-side photo comparison cards showing real transformations (Weathered Fence ➡️ Stained Wood, Dirty Concrete ➡️ Clean Driveway).

8. STREAMLINED LEAD ESTIMATE FORM & FOOTER:

   - A frictionless contact form (Name, Phone Number, Service Needed dropdown, and "Send My Free Quote" submit button).

   - Footer containing direct contact info, service areas, and copyright.

9. MOBILE OPTIMIZATION REQUIREMENT:

   - Include a sticky mobile bottom bar on small screens featuring a 1-tap "Call Now" button and a "Free Estimate" button so conversions are always within thumb's reach.
